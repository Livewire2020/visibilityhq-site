FAQ

What do I get from the free check?

A score out of 100 with a five-category breakdown, a comparison with a selected local competitor where suitable, and your three priority findings, by email.

Is it really free? What's the catch?

No catch. There's no payment step and no obligation. If you'd like more detail or help afterwards, there are two paid options. If you don't, you keep the free check.

I've been burned by SEO before.

We don't sell monthly SEO. Our paid services are one-off, with the scope and price agreed in writing before work starts, and no ranking guarantees.

How do I know the findings are right?

We base findings on what's publicly visible at the time we check: your Google profile, website, reviews and listings. Reports are dated. If you think we've got something factually wrong, tell us and we'll correct it.

How long does it take?

We'll confirm the timing by email when you submit.

Will this get me to number one on Google?

No one can promise that. We show what's affecting your visibility and what to fix first. Results also depend on Google and on your competitors.

Is this only for tradies?

We focus on tradies and local service businesses in Perth's northern suburbs. If you run another local business, request a free check and we'll tell you whether we can help.

What's the difference between the Growth Plan and Done For You?

The Growth Plan gives you the detailed assessment and a plan you carry out yourself. Done For You means we carry out agreed Google Business Profile improvements for you. Both include a 90-day re-audit.

Do you need my passwords?

No. We never ask for passwords. For Done For You, you invite us as a Manager on your Google Business Profile.

What if I don't have a Google Business Profile, or it isn't verified?

We'll check it and contact you. Profile setup is a separate service (A$249 inc. GST). Google decides whether and when a profile is verified, and you may need to complete steps Google requires.

Will I be charged monthly? Is there a contract?

No contract, and no monthly charges. The Growth Plan and Done For You are one-off. If we ever discuss ongoing help, it would be month-to-month and agreed in writing first.

What happens after 90 days?

We email you to arrange your re-audit, repeat the original checks and compare the results. Then we explain what's improved and what, if anything, is worth doing next. Your re-audit is completed within 7 days after the 90-day mark, once you reply to confirm. If we haven't heard back by then, a re-audit is available as a paid one-off.

Which businesses do you work with?

Trades and small local businesses in Perth's northern suburbs. If you're elsewhere, ask us.

How we score

Your Visibility Check scores five categories out of 20, for a total out of 100. It's our assessment of visibility factors at the date we check. It is not a Google score, a ranking, or a prediction of calls or customers.

  • Google Business Profile (/20): category accuracy, profile completeness, photos, posting activity, and hours and attributes.
  • Website (/20), our "Website and Enquiry Readiness" check: whether your phone number is visible and tap-to-call, whether there's a clear contact or quote path, whether your services are explained clearly, whether your service areas are easy to identify, how well the site works on a phone, whether your details match your Google profile, and whether trust information (licences, qualifications, reviews) is visible.
  • Reviews (/20): how many, average rating, how recent, whether you reply, and whether reviews mention your services.
  • Social (/20): Facebook and Instagram presence, posting recency and engagement.
  • Local listings (/20): whether your name, address and phone match on sample directories.

Competitor comparison: we assess a selected local competitor on the same five categories on the same day.

What the labels mean: 0–39 Needs attention · 40–69 Room to improve · 70–100 In good shape

Finding labels: Fix first (affects accuracy, access or customer contact) · Worth considering (could help, less urgent) · Optional. Findings are prioritised by impact, not only by the lowest score.

Limits: it's a snapshot. Profiles and search results change, so we repeat the same checks at your 90-day re-audit.
