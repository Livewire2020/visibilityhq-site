Privacy policy

Who we are

VisibilityHQ, ABN 45 048 664 541, Perth, Western Australia. Contact: jeff@visibilityhq.com.au.

What we collect

  • When you request a check, plan or service: your name, business name, email, suburb, website, trade and service areas; optionally your mobile number, Google Business Profile link and answers to our intake questions.
  • For paid work: payments are handled by PayPal, and we don't store card numbers. We keep invoice records.
  • For Done For You and profile setup: Manager or Admin access to your Google Business Profile (never your password) and any photos you send.
  • Public information about your business that we look at to prepare your check, such as your Google profile, website, reviews, listings and social pages, including dated screenshots.
  • Messages you send us.

How we use it

To prepare and deliver your check, plan or service; to contact you about it, including your 30-day check-in and your 90-day re-audit email; to invoice you and keep records; and to improve our service. If you give us a mobile number, we may call or text you about your request. Any marketing-style message identifies us and gives you a way to opt out.

How we handle your information

We handle personal information carefully and follow good privacy practice, such as collecting only what we need, keeping access limited and deleting records when we no longer need them. This is our own practice and isn't a claim that the Australian Privacy Principles apply to us.

We don't sell your details

We don't use your name, photos or results publicly without your written permission.

Who handles information for us

We use these services to run the business: Netlify (website hosting and form submissions); Google (email, documents and spreadsheets, and the access you give us to your Google Business Profile); PayPal (invoices and payments); Loom (video walkthroughs); and VentraIP (domain and DNS). Some of them store information outside Australia. We update this list if we add other tools.

Access to your Google profile

Limited to the agreed work. We remove ourselves as a Manager when the work is complete unless you ask us to stay.

Analytics and cookies

This website doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider, Netlify, may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit the site or send a form. The site loads its fonts from Google, so your IP address is also shared with Google when you visit. If we add analytics, we'll update this page.

How long we keep it

  • Invoices and transaction records: 5 years, as business tax records.
  • Client reports and baseline evidence: 2 years after your last service, so re-audits and queries can be handled. After that they are deleted, or earlier if you ask and the law allows.
  • Enquiries that don't become clients: deleted after 12 months.

Security

We use two-step verification on our key accounts and screen lock and disk encryption on the computer we work from. We only ever access your Google Business Profile through a Manager or Admin invitation, never your password. If a breach is likely to cause serious harm, we'll tell you.

Your choices

Ask to see or correct the information we hold, to have it deleted (except records we must keep), or to stop receiving messages. Email jeff@visibilityhq.com.au. We'll reply within 30 days.

Complaints

Contact us first at jeff@visibilityhq.com.au and we'll respond within 14 days. If you're not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Changes

We'll post changes on this page. Last updated: 24 November 2026.
